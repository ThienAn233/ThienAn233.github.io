A 30-page reference following the 32 main lectures of **Claudio Landim's measure theory course at IMPA**. These notes collect definitions and mathematical statements with explicit hypotheses, without proofs.

Topics include measures, measurable functions, integration, convergence theorems, product measures, and function spaces. The PDF includes a contents page, notation guide, and source references.
